Clinical Relevance:
MFCC features capture the power spectrum of the audio representation. The most prominent bands indicated above strongly correlate with micro-tremors, phonation irregularities, and spectral noise typically observed in Parkinsonian speech patterns, serving as the dominant weights pushing the CNN toward a positive diagnosis.
